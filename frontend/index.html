Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Everything you need to know about the SVGP Student No-Dues & Certificate System

Students log in directly using their official Diploma PIN (e.g. 23018-CM-001) and Full Name as registered with the college. Student name matching is case-insensitive. Your details must already exist in the college master records imported by the Clerk.
After logging into the Student Portal, click "Submit No-Dues Clearance Request". The system automatically routes your request to all 8 Common College Departments (Library, Accounts, Hostel, etc.) plus your specific branch laboratory incharges.
If an incharge records a pending book, lab equipment, or fee due, your card status changes to Due Found with the specific violation reason and contact location. You must physically resolve the item with the respective incharge. Once cleared, they will approve it online.
Students can re-notify an incharge once every 48 hours for pending clearance requests to ensure prompt administrative review without spamming.
Faculty incharges review clearance submissions for their assigned laboratory or department, issue official dues with fee amounts and violation notes, and grant 1-click approvals when all criteria are met.
The Clerk manages the master student database via Excel imports, records physical department approvals (e.g. Central Library), performs final academic verification, locks student records, and officially generates/prints Transfer and Conduct Certificates.
No. Official Transfer and Conduct Certificates are legal institutional documents issued exclusively by the Administrative Clerk and Principal after full verification and seal affixation. Students can view their verified dossier status online.
If there is a discrepancy in your name, father's name, DOB, or admission number, visit the College Administrative Office physically with original Board marksheets and admission receipts to have the Clerk update your master record.
For technical issues or feedback regarding the online portal, please email our administrative support at feedback@example.com or contact the SVGP Administrative Office during college working hours (10:00 AM - 5:00 PM).
