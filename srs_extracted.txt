1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose

This SRS specifies a real-time, browser-based college system for managing student No-Dues clearance, department-wise approvals, certificate data verification, and generation of the Transfer Certificate (TC) and Study & Conduct Certificate. It is intended for actual deployment and use rather than only local demonstration.

1.2 Scope

The system has three roles: Student, Faculty/Department Incharge, and Clerk. Student master records are imported by the Clerk from Excel into PostgreSQL. Students register against an existing imported record, submit No-Dues requests, monitor live/current status, and view certificate information read-only. Online departments process requests through faculty accounts. Physical departments are completed physically and recorded by the Clerk. After No-Dues completion, the Clerk verifies and locks certificate data and generates certificates.

1.3 Technology and Deployment

Production deployment is explicitly defined as: GitHub Pages hosts the static frontend; Render Web Service runs the Node.js + Express.js backend/API; and Render PostgreSQL stores application data. The frontend never connects directly to PostgreSQL. All database access occurs through the backend API.

1.4 Definitions

PIN: Unique student identifier and primary key, e.g. 24018-CM-044.

T. No.: Last three PIN characters with leading zeros removed.

No-Dues: Department-wise clearance process before certificate generation.

Clerk: Single administrative role with full system permissions.

Faculty Incharge: Single active account responsible for one department's online No-Dues processing.

Physical Department: Department whose approval is completed physically and recorded by the Clerk.

Final Verification: Clerk review after which certificate data is locked.

Certificate Version: A generated certificate revision; superseded versions remain internal records.

2. Overall Description

2.1 User Roles

Student: Registers with Name + PIN + Department; logs in with Name + PIN; submits No-Dues; monitors department status; views certificate fields read-only.

Faculty Incharge: Receives department requests/notifications; sees necessary student information; adds, clears and approves department dues/No-Dues.

Clerk: Full administrative access to Excel import, student records, faculty accounts, departments, physical approvals, certificate correction, final verification, locking, generation and version records.

3. Functional Requirements

FR-01: The Clerk shall upload student data using Excel.

FR-02: The system shall validate every Excel row before modifying the database.

FR-03: All mandatory fields required by the actual TC shall be present in valid imported rows.

FR-04: An empty or invalid mandatory field rejects the entire row; partial records shall not be saved.

FR-05: The validation preview shall show total, valid, rejected, new and existing/update rows.

FR-06: After Clerk confirmation, valid rows shall be inserted or updated; an existing PIN identifies the existing student record.

FR-07: Rejected rows and validation reasons shall be displayed on the website; no rejected-row Excel export is required.

FR-08: DOB and Date of Admission shall use DD-MM-YYYY.

FR-09: Date of Leaving shall be a text/string value entered by the Clerk during certificate generation, such as May/June 2026.

FR-10: Any imported student may register.

FR-11: Registration requires Name + PIN + Department.

FR-12: The system shall verify that the PIN exists and Name and Department match the imported record.

FR-13: Name matching shall be case-insensitive; PIN matching shall be exact.

FR-14: Department shall be selected from a dropdown.

FR-15: No student password shall be required.

FR-16: Student login shall require Name + PIN.

FR-17: Any registered student may submit a No-Dues request at any time.

FR-18: Students shall see current department-wise No-Dues progress.

FR-19: Each Online department shall have one active faculty incharge account.

FR-20: Physical departments shall be completed physically and recorded by the Clerk.

FR-21: When a due is found, the student shall see the department, exact reason and instruction to contact the responsible Lab Incharge physically.

FR-22: Faculty shall add dues using free-text reasons.

FR-23: Faculty shall be able to clear an individual due or clear all dues in their department.

FR-24: After dues are cleared, the existing No-Dues request shall be re-checkable without a new request.

FR-25: Faculty shall approve the department when no active due remains.

FR-26: Students may re-notify a pending faculty request once every 48 hours.

FR-27: No additional escalation workflow is required; the student may contact the responsible person physically.

FR-28: Faculty dashboards shall show actionable information plus simple statistics such as pending requests, active dues, approved requests and cleared dues.

FR-29: New requests shall create an in-dashboard notification and notification badge/count.

FR-30: Faculty shall see only information necessary to process No-Dues, including Name, PIN, Admission No., Department, request status/date and department dues.

FR-31: Faculty shall only act on their assigned department.

FR-32: The Clerk dashboard shall show college-wide statistics and important pending actions.

FR-33: There shall be one Clerk role with full Clerk permissions.

FR-34: Clerk authentication shall use username + password + device biometric authentication where supported.

FR-35: Raw biometric data shall not be stored; device-backed WebAuthn/passkey authentication should be used.

FR-36: Clerk shall create, activate/deactivate and manage faculty accounts, including department, role, username and initial password.

FR-37: Faculty usernames shall be flexible unique strings with no forced naming pattern.

FR-38: Faculty password reset shall be performed by the Clerk; no self-service reset is required.

FR-39: Clerk shall create/manage departments and configure Online or Physical No-Dues handling.

FR-40: Department credentials represent the incharge position; a new incharge may receive the existing department credentials.

FR-41: After No-Dues becomes Completed, certificate information shall become visible to the student.

FR-42: Student certificate information shall be read-only.

FR-43: If information is incorrect, the student shall contact the Clerk physically; no online correction-request feature is required.

FR-44: Clerk shall be able to correct information before final verification.

FR-45: Clerk shall perform final verification of all certificate data.

FR-46: Final Verify shall lock certificate data.

FR-47: An explicit Edit/Unlock action shall be required for post-verification corrections; data must be verified and locked again before generation.

FR-48: Conduct & Character shall be one field with values such as Good, Satisfactory and Poor, plus Other. Other permits custom Clerk wording; it is not an additional separate field.

FR-49: The same Conduct & Character value shall be used for TC and Study & Conduct Certificate.

FR-50: Fees Paid shall be a Yes/No dropdown.

FR-51: Promotion/qualification status shall be a free-text field.

FR-52: Only fields confirmed from the actual certificate designs shall be implemented.

FR-53: Certificate generation date shall automatically be the date on which the Clerk generates the certificate.

FR-54: The applicable Study & Conduct certificate date shall use the same generation date.

FR-55: Admission From shall use imported Date of Admission.

FR-56: Admission To shall use the Clerk-entered Date of Leaving string.

FR-57: T. No. shall be derived from the last three PIN characters with leading zeros removed.

FR-58: TC and Study & Conduct Certificate shall be generated only from final verified data.

FR-59: Each generated certificate shall have a generation record, date and version.

FR-60: Corrections after generation shall follow unlock → correction → final verification → new version.

FR-61: Superseded certificate versions shall remain internal records only and shall not be exposed to students as current/downloadable certificates.

FR-62: The latest generated version shall be the current certificate version.

5. Business Rules

PIN values are unique.

Invalid/incomplete imported rows are rejected as complete rows.

Student registration is possible only when the student already exists in imported data.

Registration = Name + PIN + Department; login = Name + PIN.

Name matching is case-insensitive; PIN matching is exact.

Any registered student may request No-Dues.

Faculty can act only for their assigned department.

Physical department approval is recorded by the Clerk.

Re-notification is available once every 48 hours while pending.

Students cannot directly edit official certificate data.

Final verification and locking are required before certificate generation.

Corrections after generation create a new version while retaining the old version internally.

Students see current/final certificate status, not superseded versions.

6. Real-Time Deployment Architecture

The production architecture shall be:

GitHub Pages — Static frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and frontend assets.

Render Web Service — Node.js + Express.js backend, REST APIs, authentication/authorization and business logic.

Render PostgreSQL — Production relational database for students, faculty, departments, No-Dues, notifications, certificate data and generation/version records.

Data flow:

Browser → GitHub Pages frontend → HTTPS API → Render Node.js/Express backend → Render PostgreSQL

The browser shall never connect directly to PostgreSQL. Database credentials shall remain server-side in Render environment variables/secrets.

6.1 Real-Time Behaviour

New No-Dues submissions shall become available to the responsible faculty through the backend/database workflow.

Faculty actions shall update the PostgreSQL record and the student's next dashboard refresh shall show the latest status.

Notification badges/counts shall reflect current pending requests.

The 48-hour Re-notify control shall be calculated from the stored request/notification timestamp.

Certificate generation shall use the latest verified and locked database values.

7. Non-Functional Requirements

Security: Backend-enforced role authorization, secure password hashing, HTTPS in production, protected secrets, server-side validation.

Privacy: Student sees only their own records; faculty sees only necessary department-related information.

Integrity: PostgreSQL constraints and backend validation protect PIN uniqueness, mandatory data and workflow state.

Availability: System should remain accessible through the deployed services during normal operation.

Performance: Common dashboard/API operations should respond promptly under expected college usage.

Responsive UI: Support modern desktop and mobile browsers.

Maintainability: Keep frontend, backend routes/controllers/services and database modules separated and maintainable.

Auditability: Retain certificate generation/version records and important administrative state changes.

Usability: Minimal role-focused UI; avoid unnecessary controls and avoid generic white/blue-heavy common UI styling.

Backup/Recovery: Production PostgreSQL data should use the hosting platform's available backup/recovery facilities and a defined operational backup policy before real college use.

8. Security Requirements

All role permissions shall be enforced on the backend.

Students may access only their own data.

Faculty may modify only dues belonging to their assigned department.

Clerk-only operations require Clerk authorization.

Passwords must never be stored in plaintext.

HTTPS must be used for deployed frontend/API communication.

Excel and API inputs must be validated server-side.

Use parameterized queries or safe database/ORM mechanisms.

Raw biometric information must not be stored.

Certificate generation must use verified/locked data.

Database connection strings and secrets must be stored as server-side environment variables, not in GitHub Pages frontend code.

9. UI/UX Requirements

Design shall be minimal and role-specific rather than using a generic login/dashboard template.

Do not use the common white-and-blue visual combination as the default design direction.

College logo and college/branch picture shall use configurable placeholders.

Logo/picture configuration should be centralized so replacing the image once updates all pages that use it.

Do not repeat college imagery on every page unnecessarily.

Buttons and controls shall reflect the actual responsibilities of each role.

Student, Faculty and Clerk interfaces shall not expose controls belonging to other roles.

10. Explicitly Out of Scope

Online payment or online settlement of dues.

Online student correction-request workflow.

Separate rejected-row Excel export.

Multiple Clerk permission levels.

Multiple simultaneous faculty incharges per department.

Student passwords.

Automatic escalation beyond the 48-hour re-notification/physical-contact process.

Student access to superseded certificate versions.

Inventing certificate fields not present on the actual college forms.

11. Final Certificate Mapping

The actual supplied TC, Study & Conduct Certificate and No-Dues designs are the authoritative source for final field mapping. Before implementation, every certificate field shall be mapped to its display label, database field, data type, source (imported/system/Clerk), validation rule and certificate position. This prevents accidental omission or invention of fields.

12. End-to-End Acceptance Criteria

Excel validation previews all rows before database modification.

Invalid rows are rejected completely and reasons are shown on the website.

Valid confirmed rows are inserted/updated in Render PostgreSQL.

Student registration validates Name + PIN + Department against imported data.

Student login works with Name + PIN using case-insensitive name matching.

Any registered student can submit No-Dues.

Faculty receives notifications and can manage only their department.

Due reasons are free text; individual and Clear All actions are supported.

Students see department, due reason and physical-contact instruction.

Re-notification is available every 48 hours while pending.

Physical department approval can be recorded by the Clerk.

After No-Dues completion, certificate fields become read-only for the student.

Clerk can correct, final-verify, lock and generate certificates.

Certificate generation records the current version and generation date.

Superseded versions remain internal records only.

Production communication follows GitHub Pages → Render backend → Render PostgreSQL; PostgreSQL is never exposed directly to the browser.

13. Implementation Note

This SRS is the baseline requirements document. The next implementation artifact should be a final database schema/data dictionary and API specification derived from these requirements and the exact supplied certificate forms. Any new requirement should be recorded as a controlled change rather than silently changing the workflow.
